What is holding the grade back

    Your grades

    Most things start at S and climb as grades come back. LG 0 is the exception: it is scored on the second‑lowest of the grades you actually have, so its rows start unmarked and stay out of the sum until they are marked.

    How the grade is worked out

    Each grade is worth points

    P Proficient2
    D Developing1
    S Starting0
    A Application exam4
    B Baseline exam3
    T Attempted exam1

    zyBooks is entered out of 10: 9.0 and up is P, 7.0 to 8.9 is D, below 7.0 is S.

    Subgoals take the second-highest grade

    Sort the grades and read off the second one down. One bad grade among several does not sink a subgoal.

    • One grade → that grade
    • Two or more → second-highest

    Except LG 0, which takes the second-lowest

    All four LG 0 subgoals are read from the other end — the second grade up from the bottom, counting only the ones actually graded. Miss one studio and your Collaboration rating holds; miss two and it moves. Professional skills have to hold up all semester, not just twice.

    Two things follow. An LG 0 subgoal starts at P and can only be held or pulled down, never lifted by one more good grade. And a row nobody has marked yet is left unmarked rather than assumed S, because an assumed S here is a real grade dragging the rating down.

    Global assessments take the lowest grade

    A goal’s global assessments are scored on their weakest result, except that an S is relaxed to a D. A goal with no global assessments counts as P.

    A learning goal is only as strong as its weakest part

    • Every subgoal and the global at P → the goal is P
    • Any S anywhere → the goal is S
    • Anything else → the goal is D

    What each exam level takes

    • B Baseline, 3 points — 80% on the multiple‑choice questions.
    • A Application, 4 points — satisfactory work on the free‑response question, roughly an A on it.
    • T Attempted, 1 point — a good‑faith effort that does not reach baseline.

    Three exams, so 12 of the 30 points are here — more than any single learning goal.

    LG 0 gates the A range

    28, 29 and 30 points earn A‑, A and A+ only when LG 0 (Professional Skills) is P. Otherwise the grade stops at B+, however many points you have.

    Special Topics adds up to 2

    A separate opportunity, rated P/D/S and added to your total rather than to any learning goal, so a perfect record comes to 32. Anything at or above 30 is still an A+.

    Points become letters

    Anything below 20 points is an F. If you are taking the course Pass/No Pass, C‑ (22 points) is the lowest passing grade.
